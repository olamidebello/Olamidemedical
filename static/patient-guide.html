Get an account

Ask your hospital to register you and issue a patient invitation linked to your record. Open the invitation through the approved process, choose your own username and a password of at least 12 characters, and keep the invitation and password private. There is no shared default patient login.

See your information

Sign in and use the patient navigation to view available appointments, medications, final results, care plans, invoices and messages. Access is limited to your linked patient record and permitted information. Ask your care team to explain results or missing information; do not change treatment based only on a portal result.

Appointments and messages

Request an appointment or send a message using the available forms. Check the status and contact your hospital if confirmation is needed. The portal is not continuously monitored and is not an emergency service. For an emergency use your local emergency service or urgent care route.

Video visits

Use a private location and a supported browser with microphone/camera permission. Confirm your appointment and consent with your care team. If the connection fails, contact the hospital for an alternative. Video and chat access is restricted to the assigned participants; connectivity depends on your network and server setup.

Care at another hospital

Ask authorized staff about your portability code and a time-limited sharing authorization for the receiving hospital. Sharing is not an unrestricted global search. Other hospitals can view authorized records but cannot rewrite the originating hospital chart. New care is recorded by the hospital delivering it.

Corrections and privacy requests

Contact the hospital responsible for your record to request access, an explanation, a correction or review of sharing. Signed clinical entries are corrected through traceable additions. Available rights and retention obligations depend on the hospital and applicable rules; the portal does not automatically erase a medical record.

Protect your account

Use a unique password, avoid public/shared devices when possible and sign out when finished. Do not share invitations, portability codes or screenshots publicly. Contact your hospital administrator for account recovery and report suspicious access promptly.
