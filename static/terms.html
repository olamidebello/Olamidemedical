Authorized use

Use only an account assigned to you and only for an authorized purpose within your hospital and professional scope. Keep credentials private. Do not attempt to bypass permissions, access unrelated patient records, introduce malicious content or disrupt service. Hospital administrators control local access and provider activation.

Clinical responsibility

The platform supports documentation and coordination. Qualified healthcare professionals remain responsible for clinical decisions, verification, consent and follow-up. AI responses are unverified drafts requiring professional review. The platform is not an emergency response service.

Records and sharing

Share records only through authorized processes and appropriate consent or other applicable authority. Do not upload information you are not authorized to process. These terms do not transfer ownership of patient records to the software brand.
