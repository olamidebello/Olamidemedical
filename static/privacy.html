Privacy notice — draft for operator approval

Developer guide · Staff and administrator guide · Patient guide · Terms of use · Privacy notice · Copyright and attribution

Status and contact

Document version: 17 October 2026. This implementation notice is a draft, not a claim of regulatory certification. Before production use the operator must publish its legal identity, privacy contact, applicable jurisdiction, processing purposes and authority, retention periods, hosting locations and configured service providers. Ask your hospital administrator for the responsible operator and privacy contact.

Information handled

Depending on the enabled workflows, the platform stores account and professional details; patient demographics and clinical records; appointments and visit chat; staff schedules and attendance; insurance, invoices and assistance records; device observations; sharing authorizations; and audit events. Passwords, session tokens, invitations and device credentials are stored using hashes where implemented. Infrastructure may also record network and access logs.

Use and access

The application uses information to authenticate users, manage hospital care and administration, support authorized sharing and record activity. Role and hospital boundaries restrict application access. Platform administrators manage hospitals/accounts without general chart access. This does not prevent separately privileged server operators or backup administrators from accessing stored data.

Patient mobility

Authorized staff can share selected records with a receiving hospital using an exact portability code and an expiring grant. Source hospital charts remain read-only to the receiving hospital. Revocation limits future platform access; it cannot recall information already seen or lawfully retained by a recipient.

AI, devices and telemedicine

AI requests send user-entered de-identified text and selected published knowledge context to OpenAI when configured and enabled. The app does not automatically retrieve patient charts for AI. Requests use store:false, which is not a promise of zero provider retention. Do not enter patient identifiers. Device submissions are stored for review. Telemedicine uses authenticated signaling/chat and browser media; configured TURN relays may carry encrypted media traffic. The app has no built-in call recording feature.

Cookies and external processing

The application uses a session cookie for sign-in. No advertising or analytics trackers are included in the supplied application. Operators must separately disclose any trackers, hosting, relays, gateways or integrations they add, including destinations and relevant transfers.

Storage, retention and requests

Database files, backups, logs and infrastructure require operator-defined protection and retention. No universal automatic deletion schedule is implemented. Contact the hospital responsible for your record for access, correction, sharing review or other privacy requests. The operator must explain applicable rights, verification, complaint routes and any lawful retention limits. Clinical corrections preserve signed originals.

Security and changes

Report suspected exposure through your hospital security contact. Operators must implement incident response and any required notifications, review deployed behavior against this notice, and publish changes with an effective date. This software alone does not establish compliance with any healthcare privacy law.
