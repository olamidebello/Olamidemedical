Sign in and find work

Use the account issued by your hospital. Search the sidebar for a workspace. Your role determines the buttons and records available. Select named patients and linked records carefully; refresh if a version conflict reports that someone else changed the record. Sign out on shared computers.

Hospital onboarding

Request hospital registration from the sign-in page. Platform administrators review requests, create hospital workspaces and issue administrator invitations. Platform administrators manage hospitals and accounts, without global clinical chart access. Hospital administrators manage only their own staff and records.

Register patients and staff

Create a patient record with verified demographics and contact details. Check possible duplicates before creating another chart. Hospital administrators use registration and user management to issue patient or employee invitations. Invitations expire after seven days and should be sent through an approved private channel.

Register and manage providers

Providers apply from the sign-in page with the hospital code, contact information and license details. Hospital administrators review evidence and record a reference. Approval issues an invitation; accepting it leaves the account disabled until explicit activation. Review current credentials before activation. Suspend or deactivate through provider management to revoke account access.

Nursing and doctor workspaces

Open My Patients, verify identity and choose the appropriate structured chart. Record the time care occurred. Save a draft, review it and sign only when accurate. Signed entries cannot be overwritten; create a linked correction with its reason. Review medication, results and outstanding tasks before handover.

Rosters and coverage

Use daily, weekly or monthly scheduling views. Assign staff, shifts and patients; check overlaps and leave. Staff record attendance and request leave or swaps. A colleague accepts a swap before administrator approval. Supervisors review unassigned patients, coverage gaps and pending handovers.

Sharing and medical devices

Patient mobility requires an exact portability code and an explicit, expiring hospital sharing authorization. External hospital charts are read-only; document your own care locally. Shared demographics have version and provenance checks. Review device identity, patient, measurement, units and time before accepting a pending observation.

Telemedicine and assistance

Schedule a visit with the assigned patient/provider, record consent and test microphone/camera. Use visit chat and media controls, then end the call. Knowledge articles and AI can help organize de-identified questions and drafts. A clinician must verify all output; AI cannot sign charts or prescribe.

Insurance, assistance and alerts

Record payer policies, authorizations, claims and manual remittances with evidence references. These records do not submit claims to payer networks. Apply approved charity sponsorships or coupons under local rules. Review alerts and critical-result queues, document acknowledgment and follow-up, and escalate urgent concerns through hospital procedures.

Get help

For access problems contact your hospital administrator. For incorrect clinical content contact the responsible care team. Report suspected unauthorized access immediately through hospital security procedures. Messages and alerts are not guaranteed emergency channels.
